Printer Settings Guide

For accurate physical ID dimensions, check these settings in your printer dialog:

  • Paper Size: A4 (210 × 297 mm)
  • Orientation: Portrait
  • Scale: Select Actual Size or 100% (do not use "Fit to Printable Area" or "Shrink to Fit").
  • Margins: Set to None or Default.
* Note: Physical printers have unprintable mechanical edge margins (~3-5 mm). The 15 mm side margins and 25.4 mm top margin keep all card content safe from hardware clipping.
